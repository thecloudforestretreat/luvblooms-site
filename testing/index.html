Service underneath

Luxury visuals need luxury steadiness.

The process has to feel organized, warm, detail-aware, and deeply dependable to earn the aesthetic.

The goal is not more cards, more blur, or more rounded corners. The goal is tension, rhythm, texture, and confidence.

Rounded corners are not the problem. Overusing one identical shape everywhere is. For this brand, the higher-end move is a disciplined system: softly rounded edges for buttons and panels, cleaner rectangles for image fields, and only enough softness to feel romantic without slipping into generic.

For the buttons specifically, the most premium direction here is not fully pill-rounded and not fully square. It’s square with softened corners: architectural, clean, and more aligned with an artsy, detail-sensitive audience.

Symmetry and alignment matter because this audience notices composition. When the structure feels intentional, the work itself feels more trustworthy and more worth investing in.
