Privacy policy.

How LuvBlooms handles information submitted through this website.

Based in Doral · Serving Miami-Dade County

Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide through inquiry forms or direct communications, such as your name, email, phone number, event or arrangement details, preferred date, location, budget range, and message.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review requests, respond to inquiries, arrange conversations, provide requested services, maintain business records, prevent misuse, and improve the website.

Analytics and attribution

On the production website, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics may collect technical and interaction information such as pages viewed, referral source, campaign parameters, device information, and approximate location. Staging is configured without production analytics.

Turnstile and service providers

Cloudflare Turnstile helps protect forms from automated abuse. Form submissions are processed through Google Apps Script. WhatsApp, email, and social links are governed by the privacy practices of those services.

Retention and security

We retain inquiry information only as long as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, recordkeeping, and legitimate business needs. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete information you submitted by emailing luvblooms.us@gmail.com. We may need to retain limited records when required for legal or legitimate business purposes.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to luvblooms.us@gmail.com.
