01 · Information

Information we collect

Information you provide. When you send a floral inquiry or contact LuvBlooms directly, we may receive your name, email address, phone number, inquiry type, preferred date, city or location, working budget range, project details, consent to be contacted, and anything else you choose to include.

Website and attribution information. The site may record the page you visited, referring page, landing page, campaign parameters such as UTM values, advertising click identifiers, event interactions, timestamps, browser or device information, and approximate location. The site also creates random visitor, session, and contact-intent identifiers to connect first-touch and recent-touch attribution without asking you to create an account.

Do not submit Social Security numbers, payment-card details, medical information, passwords, or other sensitive information through the inquiry form.

02 · Purpose

How we use information

LuvBlooms uses information to review whether a request may be a fit; respond to questions; coordinate a meeting or next step; provide requested floral services; document communications and business activity; troubleshoot and secure the website; measure page and campaign performance; and improve content, navigation, and forms.

Submitting the form authorizes LuvBlooms to contact you about that inquiry. It does not subscribe you to a general marketing list, reserve a date, confirm availability, or create a client relationship.

03 · Measurement

Analytics, attribution, and browser storage

On the production website, Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics are configured to measure visits and interactions. These tools may use cookies or similar browser technologies and may receive technical information such as pages viewed, approximate geography, device or browser characteristics, referral source, and interactions with buttons, forms, language controls, or outbound links.

LuvBlooms also stores attribution information in your browser using local storage and session storage. This may include campaign parameters, click identifiers, first and recent landing pages, referrer, capture time, and randomly generated visitor or session identifiers. You can clear or block browser storage through your browser settings, although doing so may limit attribution continuity.

The staging website does not load production analytics identifiers. Production analytics is used to understand aggregate performance, not to make automated decisions about you.

04 · Website services

Turnstile and service providers

Cloudflare supports site delivery and provides Turnstile, which evaluates signals needed to distinguish legitimate visitors from automated abuse. Google Apps Script processes inquiry submissions. Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics support measurement and attribution on the production site.

If you choose links to WhatsApp, email, Google Reviews, or social platforms, your interaction with those services is governed by their own privacy terms. LuvBlooms does not control how an external platform handles information after you leave this website.

05 · Disclosure

When information may be shared

LuvBlooms does not use this website to sell personal information. Information may be available to service providers that operate the website, process form submissions, protect against abuse, measure performance, or support communications. These providers handle information under their own terms and technical safeguards.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to a valid legal request, protect rights or safety, investigate misuse, or complete a business transition. We do not publicly display inquiry details without permission.

06 · Stewardship

Retention and security

Inquiry and communication records are kept only as long as reasonably needed to respond, evaluate or deliver services, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or satisfy legal obligations. Analytics retention is controlled through the applicable Google property settings, and browser-stored attribution data remains until it is cleared, expires through browser behavior, or is removed by the site.

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures, including encrypted HTTPS delivery and automated-abuse protection. No website, email, transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

07 · Your choices

Access, correction, and deletion requests

You may ask whether LuvBlooms holds information you submitted and request access, correction, or deletion by emailing luvblooms.us@gmail.com. Please provide enough context to locate the relevant inquiry. We may take reasonable steps to verify the request.

Some information may be retained when reasonably required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, recordkeeping, or the establishment or defense of legal claims. You can also limit cookies or clear site data through your browser and use available controls offered by Google or other external services.

08 · Changes

Children and policy updates

This website is intended for adults seeking floral services and is not directed to children. LuvBlooms does not knowingly request personal information from children through the inquiry form.

This policy may change as the website, service providers, or business practices evolve. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current version. Material changes will be reflected here.
