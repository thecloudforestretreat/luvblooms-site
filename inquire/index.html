Frequently asked questions

Before you inquire.

Which inquiry type should I choose?

Choose Brand Events and Activations for company-led work. Choose Arrangement Request for a custom arrangement, intimate event, floral consultation, or interest in the future at-home experience.

What information should I include in my floral inquiry?

Include the date if known, Miami-Dade location, occasion or project, approximate scale or quantity, desired atmosphere, working budget, and any delivery or on-site styling needs.

What happens after I submit the form?

LuvBlooms reviews the date, location, service type, scope, and budget, then contacts you to arrange a conversation when the request and availability may align.

Does submitting an inquiry confirm my date?

No. The form starts a conversation only. Availability, scope, pricing, and the date are not confirmed until LuvBlooms reviews the request and reaches an agreement with you.

Is a working budget required?

A budget is not required to send the form, but sharing a realistic range helps LuvBlooms evaluate scope and recommend an appropriate direction.

Does LuvBlooms accept wedding inquiries?

No. LuvBlooms does not offer wedding floral design. The inquiry form is for brand events and activations, intimate events, custom arrangements, and floral design consultations.
