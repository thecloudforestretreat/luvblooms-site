Why LuvBlooms

Personal enough to feel meaningful. Composed enough to feel effortless.

We begin with the feeling the flowers should create, then shape palette, scale, form, movement, and placement around the setting. A brand launch needs a different kind of attention than a private dinner or a meaningful arrangement, so each project is developed on its own terms.

Our approach combines editorial restraint with practical planning. We consider how flowers will be encountered in person, how they relate to the architecture and tablescape, and how the composition will read in photographs.

A useful starting point

Tell us what the flowers need to accomplish.

You do not need a finished floral brief before contacting us. A date, location, approximate scope, working budget, and a few words about the desired feeling are enough to begin. References such as invitations, brand guidelines, room photographs, or a color palette can help us understand the context.

Helpful details

  • Date, location, and preferred delivery or setup window
  • Type of event, arrangement, or consultation
  • Approximate guest count, quantity, or areas to style
  • Working budget and any visual references already available

How it works

A clear path from idea to flowers.

The inquiry is intentionally concise. Once we understand the essentials, we can decide whether the request is a fit and identify the right next conversation.

  1. Choose Brand Events and Activations or Arrangement Request, then share the date, location, budget, and desired atmosphere.
  2. We review the request, clarify priorities, and contact you to arrange a focused conversation when the project is a fit.
  3. After the direction and scope are confirmed, we outline the floral approach and practical production steps.

Frequently asked questions

Helpful details before you inquire.

What floral design services does LuvBlooms offer?

LuvBlooms creates floral design for brand events and activations, intimate events, custom arrangements, and focused floral design consultations. Curated at-home floral experiences are coming soon.

Does LuvBlooms design weddings?

No. LuvBlooms does not offer wedding floral design. Our studio remains focused on brand experiences, intentionally sized gatherings, personal arrangements, and consultations.

Where does LuvBlooms provide service?

LuvBlooms is based in Doral and considers projects throughout Miami-Dade County. Delivery, placement, and on-site styling availability depend on the location and project scope.

How far ahead should I inquire?

Earlier is helpful for events, installations, larger quantities, or specific sourcing. If your date is approaching, you may still inquire; availability is reviewed case by case.

Do I need to know exactly what flowers I want?

No. You can begin with the occasion, setting, color direction, mood, and budget. We use those details to guide a floral direction appropriate for the season and purpose.

How do I begin?

Use the short inquiry form and choose the request type that best fits. Share the essential details, and LuvBlooms will contact you about fit and the next step.

Have a floral moment in mind?

Share the occasion, setting, timing, and the feeling you want to create. We will review the details and guide the next step.
