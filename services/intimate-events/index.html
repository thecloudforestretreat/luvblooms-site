Made for connection

Atmosphere matters most when every detail is close.

At an intimate gathering, guests notice the relationship between the flowers, table, light, room, and one another. Floral design can establish warmth, create rhythm across the setting, and make a celebration feel complete without overwhelming its scale.

We begin with the occasion and the feeling you want guests to carry away. From there, we consider proportion, sightlines, seasonal material, color, vessels, and how each arrangement should support conversation and movement.

  • Welcome, bar, buffet, dessert, or focal floral moments
  • Feature arrangements for a host or guest of honor
  • Delivery, placement, and styling according to the confirmed scope
  • Floral direction coordinated with linens, candles, menus, or room details

The feeling of the room

Quietly expressive. Intentionally placed.

Flowers create rhythm across a table and warmth throughout a room. We consider sightlines, conversation, candlelight, and the natural movement of the gathering.

Planning the details

A smaller guest count allows every choice to be felt.

Share the date, Miami-Dade location, approximate guest count, tablescape plans, working budget, and any elements already selected. Invitations, linens, dinnerware, candles, room photographs, and color references can help us understand the visual context.

We will identify which floral moments create the greatest impact for the available scope. In some settings, one expressive focal arrangement and considered table flowers are more effective than placing flowers everywhere.

Scale with intention

“Intimate” describes the experience, not a fixed guest-count limit. The venue, number of tables, delivery conditions, and styling requirements all influence whether the project is a fit.

Process

A simple, collaborative process.

The process stays focused so the floral decisions remain proportional to the gathering.

  1. Tell us about the occasion, date, location, guest count, working budget, and the atmosphere you want to create.
  2. We clarify the priority areas, visual references, delivery or placement needs, and any practical requirements of the setting.
  3. Once the direction and scope are confirmed, we outline the floral approach and coordinate the agreed production steps.

FAQ

Questions about intimate-event florals.

What kinds of gatherings are a fit?

Private dinners, birthdays, showers, milestone celebrations, family gatherings, and other intentionally sized occasions may be a fit. We review the venue, guest count, floral needs, and logistics together.

Can you design just the dining table?

Yes. A focused tablescape can be appropriate when the table is the center of the experience. Share its size, shape, seating arrangement, linens, candles, and any serving requirements.

Can you work with a color palette or invitation?

Yes. A palette, invitation, room photograph, linen sample, dinnerware, or mood reference can guide the direction. Exact materials remain subject to seasonal availability.

Do you deliver and place the arrangements?

Delivery and placement may be available depending on date, location, access, and scope. Tell us whether someone can receive the flowers and whether on-site placement is required.

How early should I inquire?

Earlier inquiries allow more time for planning and sourcing. Smaller requests with flexible materials may sometimes be possible with shorter notice, depending on availability.

Do you offer wedding floral design?

No. LuvBlooms does not offer wedding floral design. This service is for private gatherings and celebrations outside the wedding category.
