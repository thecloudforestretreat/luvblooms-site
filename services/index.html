Choosing well

Start with the outcome, not a flower list.

If the flowers support a company, campaign, or guest-facing brand experience, begin with Brand Events and Activations. If they are for a private gathering, use Florals for Intimate Events. A standalone personal piece belongs under Custom Arrangements.

Choose Floral Design Consultations when the main need is creative clarity rather than immediate production. If the request overlaps more than one category, select the closest option and explain the context in the inquiry.

What shapes the scope

  • Purpose, date, venue, and number of floral moments
  • Scale, guest count, delivery, placement, and styling needs
  • Palette, seasonality, visual references, and brand requirements
  • Working budget and the priorities that matter most

Our scope

LuvBlooms specializes in brand events and activations, intimate events, custom arrangements, and floral design consultations. This focused scope allows us to give proportion, material, placement, and context the attention they deserve.

Which service should I choose?

Choose Brand Events for a business-led or branded experience, Intimate Events for a private gathering, and Custom Arrangements for a standalone personal piece. Choose a Consultation when you primarily need direction. If the request overlaps, select the closest option and explain.

What information should I include?

Include the date, location, type of request, approximate scale or quantity, working budget, and desired feeling. Visual references are welcome but not required.

Can LuvBlooms deliver or style flowers on site?

Delivery, placement, and on-site styling may be available depending on the service, location, timing, and scope. These requirements should be noted in the inquiry.

Does LuvBlooms serve all of Miami-Dade County?

LuvBlooms is based in Doral and considers inquiries throughout Miami-Dade County. Project logistics and any associated delivery or setup requirements are reviewed individually.

Does LuvBlooms offer wedding flowers?

No. LuvBlooms does not offer wedding floral design.
