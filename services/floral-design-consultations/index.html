Clarity before production

A useful direction before you invest in execution.

A floral design consultation helps organize the visual and practical decisions that shape a project. Together, we can examine palette, proportion, seasonal possibilities, sourcing, styling, placement, and how the flowers should relate to the setting or guest experience.

This service is for clients who need an experienced floral point of view before ordering materials or committing to production. LuvBlooms may later be considered for implementation, but the consultation is valuable as a focused creative service on its own.

Defined scope

Creative direction is different from production.

A clear boundary helps the consultation remain useful. We confirm the format, topics, and expected outcome before booking so the conversation can stay focused.

What the consultation can do

It can refine a concept, evaluate references, identify a palette, discuss seasonal materials, explore scale and placement, flag practical constraints, and clarify the decisions needed before execution.

What remains separate

Purchasing, detailed procurement, physical samples, floral production, delivery, installation, and on-site styling are not automatically included. Any later production scope is considered separately according to fit and availability.

Prepare for a useful conversation

Bring the decisions—not just the inspiration.

Share the date, Miami-Dade location, project type, approximate budget, photographs or plans of the space, existing creative direction, and any references already under consideration. Most importantly, explain which decisions feel unresolved.

Brand guidelines, invitations, menus, fabric or paint references, tablescape elements, product imagery, and venue restrictions may all be relevant. The more clearly the context is defined, the more specific the guidance can become.

A consultation may be a fit when…

You have too many ideas, the visual direction lacks cohesion, scale is difficult to judge, or you want an expert floral perspective before approving a plan.

Not a wedding-planning service

LuvBlooms does not provide wedding floral design or wedding consultation services. Our focus remains on brands, intimate events, arrangements, and considered spaces.

Process

A focused path from question to direction.

The consultation begins before the conversation, with a clear understanding of the project and the decisions that matter most.

  1. Submit an inquiry with the project, location, timeline, working budget, visual references, and the questions you need to resolve.
  2. We review the request, confirm whether a consultation is the right service, and define the appropriate scope and preparation.
  3. The consultation concentrates on the agreed priorities so you leave with a clearer direction and practical next decisions.

FAQ

Questions about floral design consultations.

Who is a consultation for?

A consultation is useful for hosts, creative teams, brands, property teams, and anyone who needs an informed floral direction before committing to production.

What questions can we cover?

Topics may include concept, palette, seasonal direction, scale, placement, sourcing, styling, and how flowers should relate to a room, table, brand, or guest experience.

What should I prepare?

Share the date, location, approximate budget, photographs or plans of the space, visual references, and the specific decisions you need to make.

What will I receive from the consultation?

The goal is a focused floral direction and clearer next decisions. The exact consultation format and any supporting material are confirmed before booking.

Is implementation included?

No. The consultation covers creative direction. Sourcing, purchasing, production, delivery, installation, and styling are separate unless explicitly included in a later scope.

Can LuvBlooms produce the design afterward?

Production may be discussed after the consultation, but it is not guaranteed and depends on the project type, date, location, scope, and availability.

Bring the question. Leave with direction.

Share the project, visual context, timeline, budget, and decisions you need to make. We will review the request and contact you about the appropriate next step.
