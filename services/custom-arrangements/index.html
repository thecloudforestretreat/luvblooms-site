Designed around the reason

Personal flowers begin with what they need to express.

A meaningful arrangement is more than a combination of flowers. It considers who will receive it, where it will live, why it is being given, and what the gesture should communicate. We use those details to create an individual composition rather than reproduce a standard catalog design.

The result may be quiet and refined, abundant and celebratory, sculptural and contemporary, or softened by garden movement. The exact flowers remain flexible so the final arrangement can respond to season, quality, palette, and the intended atmosphere.

What shapes the design

  • The recipient, occasion, message, and setting
  • Seasonal flower and foliage quality
  • Palette, vessel, scale, silhouette, and movement
  • Working budget and practical delivery requirements
  • Any visual reference used as direction—not a template

A useful request

Give us context, not a finished recipe.

Begin with the delivery or pickup date, Miami-Dade location, recipient or room, occasion, working budget, and the feeling you want the flowers to carry. If color matters, share the palette or a useful reference. If the arrangement will live in a particular place, a photograph and approximate dimensions can help us understand its surroundings.

You are welcome to mention favorite flowers, but exact varieties are never the only path to the right result. Allowing room for seasonal substitutions helps us prioritize quality, proportion, and the overall composition.

Seasonality is part of the design

Flower availability changes with season, weather, quality, and market conditions. Specific varieties cannot always be guaranteed, but preferences can guide the selection.

Delivery and pickup

Options are reviewed according to the date, destination, access, timing, size, and scope. Include the complete location details in your request whenever possible.

Process

  1. Choose Arrangement Request and share the recipient or setting, occasion, date, location, working budget, and desired feeling.

FAQ

Questions about custom arrangements.

What makes an arrangement custom?

Each piece is designed from the recipient, occasion, setting, desired feeling, palette, scale, and working budget. It is not copied from a fixed menu or standard catalog image.

Can I request exact flowers?

You may share favorite flowers, but seasonal availability and quality vary. We treat specific varieties as direction and recommend suitable alternatives when needed.

Is delivery available?

Delivery may be available in Doral and throughout Miami-Dade depending on the date, address, access, timing, and order scope. Include the complete destination in your request.

Is pickup available?

Pickup options are confirmed individually after the date, size, timing, and arrangement requirements are reviewed.

How much notice is needed?

More notice provides greater sourcing and design flexibility. Short-notice requests may still be considered according to availability, so submit the form even if the date is approaching.

What should I include in the request?

Share the date, delivery or pickup preference, recipient or setting, occasion, working budget, desired feeling, and any useful color or style references.

Tell us who or what the flowers are for.

Share the date, location, budget, occasion, and desired feeling. We will review the request and contact you about availability and the next step.
