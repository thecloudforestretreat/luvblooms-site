The idea

Flowers that feel connected to the way you live.

This future offering is being imagined for people who want flowers to become part of the atmosphere of home—not simply an occasional delivery. The intention is to create seasonal compositions with an editorial point of view, considered in relation to the room, vessel, scale, and mood.

Rather than choosing from a fixed catalog, clients would receive a LuvBlooms interpretation of what feels fresh and compelling at that moment. The exact structure is still taking shape, and interest shared now will help inform the experience.

Frequently asked questions

What to know while the experience takes shape.

What is the curated at-home floral experience?

It is a coming-soon LuvBlooms concept for bringing a considered, seasonal floral composition into the home on a recurring basis.

Is the at-home floral service available now?

Not yet. The experience is in development, and LuvBlooms has not announced a launch date.

Will this be a flower subscription?

A recurring format is being explored, but cadence, commitment options, pricing, and other subscription details are not final.

Where will the at-home experience be available?

LuvBlooms is based in Doral and is evaluating future availability across Miami-Dade County. The final service area has not been confirmed.

Can I request exact flowers or colors?

The concept is centered on seasonal curation rather than exact flower guarantees. Preferences and the character of the home may help guide the future experience.

How can I share my interest?

Use the inquiry form, choose Arrangement Request, and mention curated at-home experiences in your message. This expresses interest and is not a subscription purchase.
