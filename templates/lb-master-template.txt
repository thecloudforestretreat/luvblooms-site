============================================================
GLOBAL VISUAL DIRECTION STANDARD
============================================================

LuvBlooms should not feel like a generic florist template.
The site should feel:
- art-directed
- emotionally composed
- quiet but expensive
- romantic without being overly soft
- immersive without becoming cluttered
- editorial rather than modular

The visual goal is not "pretty."
The visual goal is desirability, trust, and atmosphere.

============================================================
DESIGN PRINCIPLES TO STANDARDIZE
============================================================

1. IMAGE-LED, NOT BOX-LED
- Let photography do major emotional work.
- Use large, immersive image fields when appropriate.
- Avoid over-framing every image in repeated boxes.
- Favor cinematic crops, elegant negative space, and layered overlays.

2. COMPOSITION OVER REPETITION
- Do not build every section as the same card grid.
- Vary rhythm between sections:
  - immersive hero
  - quieter text-led band
  - asymmetrical feature split
  - refined proof row
  - elegant CTA moment
- Luxury comes from pacing and restraint, not from stacking more components.

3. CONSISTENT BUT DISCIPLINED CORNER SYSTEM
- Do not make everything fully pill-rounded.
- Do not mix corner radii randomly.
- Preferred LuvBlooms direction:
  - buttons: soft rectangular or lightly rounded, not overly pill-shaped unless justified
  - cards/panels: restrained radius, consistent across equivalent modules
  - image fields: cleaner rectangles or gently rounded large frames
- If softness is used, it should feel intentional and architectural.

4. TEXT LEGIBILITY IS NON-NEGOTIABLE
- Any text over imagery must have enough contrast and backdrop support.
- Use veils, tonal overlays, shallow glass panels, shadow gradients, or contained caption blocks when needed.
- Never sacrifice readability for mood.

5. SCALE WITH RESTRAINT
- The site can feel bold, but should not feel zoomed in or overwhelming by default.
- Desktop should feel spacious and poised.
- Mobile should feel immersive without making type or imagery oppressive.
- Large type should be used strategically, not everywhere.

6. SYMMETRY AND ALIGNMENT MATTER
- This audience notices composition.
- Grids, image edges, baseline alignment, and consistent spacing are part of the brand perception.
- Sloppy alignment lowers the perceived quality of the floral work.

7. FEWER DEVELOPER EXPLANATIONS, MORE BRAND LANGUAGE
- Never leave internal rationale on the page.
- No copy like:
  - "this direction..."
  - "the client should feel..."
  - "concept reference..."
  - "luxury online is mostly about..."
- All visible copy must sound client-facing, brand-facing, or conversion-facing.

============================================================
BUTTON STANDARD
============================================================

Preferred button direction for LuvBlooms:
- primary buttons: softly rounded rectangular
- secondary buttons: lightly filled with very low-opacity light background
- avoid pure outline-only secondary buttons when they feel too cold or too generic
- avoid overusing fully pill-shaped buttons unless the page specifically benefits from that softness

Buttons should feel:
- refined
- confident
- easy to click
- not techy
- not trendy for trend's sake

============================================================
BACKGROUND AND PANEL STANDARD
============================================================

Do not rely on one flat background color alone.

Preferred background techniques:
- tonal veils inside the brand palette
- subtle radial light
- soft dark-to-light transitions
- shallow translucent panels
- selective shadowing behind text blocks
- layered material contrast between sections

Do not use:
- loud gradients
- decorative clutter
- random blurred blobs
- generic SaaS-style floating cards

============================================================

MOBILE / DESKTOP UX STANDARD
============================================================

Every page must:

- avoid horizontal overflow

- avoid decorative clutter that hurts scanning

Do not:
